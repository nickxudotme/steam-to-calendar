# Asset Inventory

Runtime assets live under `public/assets` and are served by the app.

- `public/assets/brand` — logo and install icons referenced by the UI or metadata.
- `public/assets/backgrounds` — page and popover backgrounds referenced by CSS.
- `public/favicon.ico` and `public/favicon.png` — root favicon files kept at the conventional browser paths.

Design source and unused concept assets live under `design/assets` and are not served by Next.js.

- `design/assets/brand-source` — logo source exports and experiments.
- `design/assets/background-source` — raw hero background variants.
- `design/assets/background-concepts` — unused generated background concepts kept for future design work.
